## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance athletic retail environment tailored for premium sports gear, functional training hardware, and technical apparel. Customer-facing interfaces are localized entirely in Catalan, projecting an authentic, contemporary European athletic presence that balances precision engineering with visceral physical motivation.

### Audience & Mood
Targeting performance athletes, urban runners, and discerning fitness enthusiasts across Catalonia and Southern Europe, the aesthetic conveys mechanical precision, uncompromising durability, and electric kinetic momentum. The interaction model avoids sterile luxury or chaotic street culture; it leans into focused velocity, tactile confidence, and nocturnal training focus.

### Design Movements & Visual Style
- **Dark-Mode Precision Performance:** Deep nocturnal backdrops with layered carbon and charcoal surfaces recreate the aesthetic of technical graphite equipment, training spaces, and aerodynamic gear.
- **Electric Accent Calibration:** Kinetic lime accents serve strictly functional purposes—directing visual momentum toward key CTAs, technical milestones, and active filter choices without degenerating into decorative noise.
- **Refined Athletic Engineering:** Tactile borders, subtle inner surface lighting, crisp structural typography, and responsive micro-elevations deliver physical assurance on every tap or click.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast tiers designed to ensure legible information hierarchy across all digital surfaces.

### Surface Architecture & Dark Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#0f1115`):** Foundational backdrop for full-bleed screens, viewports, and outer page wrappers.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#12141a`):** Section containers, sticky utility bars, and drawer backdrops.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#181b22`):** Primary card surfaces, product listing items, and modal dialog bodies.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1f232c`):** Hovered cards, nested parameter chips, segmented track selectors, and secondary wells.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#282d38`):** Inactive toggles, subtle divider lines, and elevated tooltips.

### Accent Kinetic Scale
- **Volt Primary (`#ccff00`):** Core energetic catalyst. Reserved for primary conversion buttons ("Comprar ara", "Afegeix a la cistella"), active filtering states, and critical price highlights. Primary text placed over this color must strictly use `#0f1115` to guarantee WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Volt Midtone (`#a3e635`):** Balanced secondary green used for secondary indicators, technical specifications, and hover dynamics.
- **Volt Glow (`rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.15)`): Subtle illumination for focus rings, container highlights, and active edge strokes.

### Semantic & Feedback Colors
- **Success / En Estoc (`#22c55e`):** Confirmed stock availability, sizing confirmation, and successful purchase actions.
- **Warning / Darreres Unitats (`#f59e0b`):** Low inventory notifications, fast-selling drop alerts.
- **Critical / Esgotat (`#ef4444`):** Out-of-stock states, payment validation errors, and destructive operations.
- **Technical Highlight (`#38bdf8`):** Metric indicators, dimensional sizing specs, and laboratory test markers.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Text Primary (`#f3f4f6`):** Product titles, technical data values, and header breadcrumbs.
- **Text Secondary (`#9ca3af`):** Material compositions, localized metadata, and input label tags.
- **Text Muted (`#6b7280`):** Inactive unit tags, footers, disabled variants, and secondary borders.

## Typography

The typographic balance relies on pairing **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural, energetic headlines with **Inter** for neutral, utilitarian body copy.

### Localization & Character Treatment
- **Catalan Orthography:** Special attention is applied to typography tracking to support the Catalan middot (*punt volat* as in *col·lecció*, *instal·lació*), accented uppercase characters (*À, È, É, Í, Ò, Ó, Ú*), and hyphenated pronominal clitics (*compra'l*, *prova't*).
- **Proportional Tabular Numerals:** Price labels, weight metrics (e.g., `24.5 kg`), dimensions, and inventory countdowns use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within Inter to prevent visual jumping during dynamic data shifts.
- **Display Tightening:** Headline scales apply negative letter spacing proportional to their scale to maintain athletic tension without sacrificing structural clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements a strict 8-point base spatial cadence that supports rapid visual scanning and frictionless touch accuracy during mobile athletic shopping.

### Layout Matrix & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Product listings render in a single-column or high-density 2-column grid.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1023px`):** 8-column fluid layout, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Filter drawers dock conditionally into slide-over sheets.
- **Desktop (`1024px - 1439px`):** 12-column fixed-max layout (`max-width: 1280px`), `margin-desktop: 3rem`, `gutter-desktop: 2rem`. Persistent filter navigation column with a 3-column product grid.
- **Wide Desktop (`>= 1440px`):** 12-column centered layout (`max-width: 1400px`), with optional 4-column product matrix for high-inventory browsing.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section spacing follows the large-scale scale: `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile interfaces, extending to `space-3xl` (72px) between major retail departments, banners, and technical recommendation blocks on desktop viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through **surface tiering and low-contrast perimeter highlights** rather than heavy blurred drop shadows, retaining a clean, technical athletic appearance.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Canvas elements (`#0f1115`) with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** `#181b22` bounded by an ultra-thin 1px solid stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). No drop shadow, keeping hardware edges sharp.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards):** `#1f232c` bounded by `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.35)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`, accompanied by a subtle localized glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Headers, Quick-View & Modals):** `#181b22` supported by backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` at 88% opacity), bounded by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with depth shadow: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.

### Focus Rings
Accessibility focus indicators use an offset dual-layer technique: an inner 2px transparent barrier followed by an outer 2px solid ring in Volt Primary (`#ccff00`), ensuring sharp visibility against both dark charcoal cards and pure black canvas elements.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, athletic curves: precise enough to suggest industrial equipment, yet curved enough to remain ergonomically approachable on modern touch interfaces.

### Curvature Hierarchy
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 8px):** Form controls, text inputs, technical spec badges, and table cells.
- **Interactive Modules (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Primary buttons, filter chips, cart drawers, and notification banners.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Product cards, hero showcase slides, and category feature tiles.
- **Tactile Floats (`rounded-full`):** Floating icon buttons (favorites, cart badge counts), inventory status pills, and toggle thumb nodes.

## Components

Standardized UI patterns for the digital catalog, detailing interaction states, localized copy cues, and component layouts.

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action ("Comprar ara", "Afegeix a la cistella"):** Solid Volt Primary (`#ccff00`) background, deep charcoal text (`#0f1115`), `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (14px). Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile. Corner radius: `rounded-lg`. Hover: background shifts to `#a3e635` with subtle forward translation (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action ("Veure detalls tècnics", "Guia de talles"):** `#1f232c` background with a 1px solid stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`), white text (`#f3f4f6`). Hover: stroke transforms to `#ccff00`, text brightens.
- **Ghost Action ("Filtrar", "Netejar filtres"):** Transparent background, muted text (`#9ca3af`). Hover: text shifts to `#f3f4f6` with faint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill.

### 2. Product Cards (Cataleg de Productes)
- **Container:** Background `#181b22`, 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`), border radius `rounded-xl`, padding `space-md`. Smooth transition on border color and transform over 200ms.
- **Media Well:** Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5, background `#12141a`, border radius `rounded-lg` with internal overflow hidden.
- **Variant - In Stock ("En estoc"):** Displays price in pure white (`#f3f4f6`), title in `headline-sm`, and a status pill with a pulsing green indicator (`#22c55e`). Primary action button reveals or activates seamlessly on hover/focus.
- **Variant - Out of Stock ("Esgotat"):** Media well desaturated to 40% opacity (`filter: grayscale(80%)`). Features a pinned badge: dark surface (`#12141a`) with a subtle warning/critical red tag (`#ef4444`). Primary action is replaced by a disabled secondary state button: "Avisa'm quan estigui disponible" (Notify when available).

### 3. Badges & Status Pills
- **Badge "Novetat" (New Launch):** Volt Primary background (`#ccff00`), dark charcoal text (`#0f1115`), `label-sm` uppercase, tracking +0.05em, padding `2px 8px`, `rounded-full`.
- **Badge "Edició Limitada":** Solid dark surface (`#1f232c`), 1px solid border (`#38bdf8`), cyan text (`#38bdf8`).
- **Stock Status Pill:** Embedded row with a 6px circular indicator:
  - Green (`#22c55e`): "En estoc — Enviament en 24h"
  - Amber (`#f59e0b`): "Últimes unitats disponibles"
  - Red (`#ef4444`): "Temporalment esgotat"

### 4. Filter Chips (Filtres Dinàmics)
- **Inactive Chip:** Background `#181b22`, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, text `#9ca3af`, `rounded-full`, height 36px, horizontal padding `space-md`.
- **Active Chip:** Background `rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.12)`, border 1.5px solid `#ccff00`, text `#ccff00`. Includes an inline 'x' dismissal glyph on the trailing edge.

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Search & Text Input:** Background `#12141a`, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#f3f4f6`, placeholder `#6b7280`. Padding `space-sm space-md`, border-radius `rounded-lg`. Focus state: border color `#ccff00`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #ccff00`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px x 20px container, `#12141a` base, 1.5px border `#6b7280`. Checked state: background `#ccff00`, border `#ccff00`, rendering a deep charcoal checkmark glyph.

### 6. Retail Header & Navigation Bar
- **Sticky Glass Frame:** Height 72px desktop / 60px mobile. Background `rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border bottom 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Elements:** Prominent brand wordmark on the leading edge, central navigation links ("Equipament", "Accessoris", "Entrenament", "Novetats") in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 14px, and action cluster (cerca, compte, cistella with counter pill) on the trailing edge.

### 7. Footer
- **Structure:** Multi-column grid on desktop (`space-3xl` top margin), `#12141a` base with top border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Catalan Legal & Service Links:** Dedicated columns for "Atenció al Client", "Sobre ActivePrat", "Sostenibilitat", and "Enviament i Devolucions". Bottom copyright row with tax, privacy ("Política de Privadesa"), and cookies confirmation.
